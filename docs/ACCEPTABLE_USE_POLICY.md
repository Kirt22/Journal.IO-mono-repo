# Journal.IO Usage Policy

Effective Date: April 22, 2026

This Usage Policy explains the guidelines for using Journal.IO safely,
respectfully, and in line with the Terms of Service.

## 1. Use Journal.IO Responsibly

Journal.IO is a private space for reflection, mood tracking, and supportive
habit-building. Use it only in compliance with applicable law and these
guidelines.

## 2. What Is Not Allowed

Do not:

- use Journal.IO to violate law, infringe another person's rights, harass
  others, or abuse the service
- access another person's account or data, bypass security controls, or
  impersonate another person or organization
- interfere with normal service operation or use automation that creates
  unreasonable load or security risk
- reverse engineer, scrape, or misuse Journal.IO except where applicable law
  allows it

## 3. Support and Safety

Journal.IO is not a medical, psychiatric, therapy, or crisis-response service.
Its AI-supported features speak directly and name behavioural and psychological
patterns in what you write. They do not diagnose conditions, provide treatment,
advise on medication, or replace professional care, and nothing they say is a
clinical finding.

For help with an account or the app, submit a request through:

- `https://api.journalio.app/support`

For immediate safety concerns, contact local emergency services or a trusted
support person.

## 4. Related Terms

This Usage Policy supplements the Journal.IO Terms of Service. If there is a
conflict, the Terms of Service control.
