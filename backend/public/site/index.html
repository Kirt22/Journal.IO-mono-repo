The loop

Three minutes of writing. The app does the noticing.

01

Write

Open to a blank page or a guided prompt, tag how the day felt, and close the app. Saving never waits on anything.

02

Notice

Your entries get read back to you as recurring themes and mood movement — never a score, never a verdict about who you are.

03

Carry forward

Each week ends with one small, practical thing worth trying. Not a lecture. One thing.

Ask Jade

Talk it through with Jade.

Tap the orb on your home screen and ask anything about your own history. Jade answers in plain language and draws only on what you have written.

Ask for the numbers and it renders them inline — how your mood moved this week, how often a theme comes up, when you actually write.

Premium. Conversations are saved, so you can pick one back up later.

Mind Map

See how your thoughts connect.

Eight regions, built from your own reflections. Rotate it, zoom in, tap a region to read the writing that pointed there — planning, memory, conflict, identity.

It fills in gradually as you write, so the shape you end up with is genuinely yours rather than a template.

A map of your writing, not a measurement of your brain. Nothing here is medical.

Guided reflection

Never face a blank page.

Three questions, one at a time. Something good that happened. Something that got in the way. One thing worth carrying into tomorrow.

Then, if you want, go deeper — Jade replies in a few sentences and leaves you with a single follow-up worth sitting with.

Writing freely is always free. Guided reflection is Premium after your first one.

Weekly analysis

Patterns, not verdicts.

Analysis waits until there is genuinely enough to say something. When there is, you get the themes that keep recurring, how your mood moved, and where the two seem to line up.

The wording stays careful on purpose: your entries keep pointing to something. They never tell you what you are.

Topics and moods are picked up from what you wrote — there is no tagging chore.

Home Screen widgets

Stay consistent without opening the app.

Three widgets, one habit each. Streak shows where you are. Quick Thought opens straight into a blank page. Mood Check-in is a single tap and never launches anything.

Streaks inside the app work the same way — a record of showing up, not a thing to lose.

The Streak widget is free. Quick Thought and Mood Check-in are Premium.

Trust

Your journal should feel like it's yours.

Journaling only works if you're honest, and you're only honest if you feel safe. So the controls are real ones, and they take effect immediately.

Face ID and Touch ID lock

The app covers itself the moment it leaves the foreground, with a short grace window so switching apps for a second doesn't nag you.

Hide journal previews

Mask entry text everywhere it would otherwise be readable over your shoulder — including inside the Mind Map.

Export and delete, for real

Take everything with you, or delete your account outright from inside the app. No email thread, no retention dark pattern.

No ads, ever

Journal.IO is paid for by the people who use it. Your entries are not a product and are never sold.

Writing never waits on AI

Analysis runs after the fact and separately. If it fails or you skip it, your entry is still saved exactly as you wrote it.

Non-clinical by design

Journal.IO is not a medical product and does not diagnose. Everything the AI says is supportive, hedged, and honest about being uncertain.
