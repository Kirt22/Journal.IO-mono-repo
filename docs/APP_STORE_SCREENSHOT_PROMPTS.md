# v2 App Store screenshots — what to do

> ## Read this once
>
> **You attach nothing to GPT Image 2. Ever.** It only gets text.
> Your app screenshots never go near it — I composite those in afterwards.
>
> Each step below makes an **empty background** with an icon and a headline.

>

# Reference

Background detail, kept out of the way of the steps above.

## Why nothing gets attached

Apple guideline 2.3.3 requires screenshots to show the **real app**. If GPT
Image 2 ever sees your UI it will redraw it, and a redrawn UI is exactly what
gets rejected. So the model only ever makes an empty backdrop, and the genuine
captures from `frontend/Screenshots/` get layered on afterwards. Two pipelines
that only meet at compositing time.

## Why 1024 × 1536 and not the real size

Final panels are 1320 × 2868 (ratio 0.46). The model's tallest portrait is 2:3
(ratio 0.67), so the art is not the final shape. During compositing it gets
placed on a 1320 × 2868 canvas anchored top at full width, and the remaining
~888px at the bottom is filled with the colour sampled from the art's bottom
edge. The gradient is smooth, so the join is invisible — and the phone covers
most of it anyway.

## Expect retries

Image models drop and duplicate letters constantly. Three or four generations
per panel before one is clean is normal. A typo shipped to the App Store costs a
resubmission, so proofread each headline character by character.

## Which numbers are rules and which are taste

Almost everything in the prompts is a **design choice, not an Apple rule** — the
18% icon placement, the 30% headline start, the 10% icon width, the glow
opacities. They exist to make six panels feel like one system. Bend them
wherever the artwork looks better.

Only two things are actually enforced:

- **1320 × 2868 exactly** (2064 × 2752 for iPad) — App Store Connect rejects
  wrong dimensions at upload. This is a validator, not a preference.
- **Guideline 2.3.3** — screenshots must show the real app.

## Device compositing (my side)

**Panels 1, 2, 3, 4, 6:** one iPhone, dead straight-on, no tilt, no rotation, no
perspective, no second device. Light-mode capture — the cream `#FDFCFB` UI
against the dark plate is what makes the product the hero. Top of the device
~56% down the canvas, bottom bleeding off the edge, identical position and scale
on all five.

**Panel 5:** same top edge and bleed, but the device scaled ~1.15× so the visible
window falls across roughly the top half of the phone with the single streak
widget large and centred.

**Separation:** a drop shadow does nothing on a dark background. A 1.5px inner rim
highlight in `#E8745F` at ~25% opacity plus a wide coral ambient glow (~15%
opacity, 200px blur) behind the device.

**Orientation:** straight-on for all six. Panel 5 forces it — a tilted iOS Home
Screen looks broken — and tilting the other five would make panel 5 the odd one
out. Tilt also costs legibility at thumbnail size, which is the exact problem
with the current live set.

## iPad

The live listing already carries `APP_IPAD_PRO_3GEN_129` (5 × 2064 × 2752), so
this is an update, not new work. Ratio 0.75 is closer to the model's native 2:3,
so generation is easier — but re-lay the composition rather than stretching the
iPhone plates: icon and headline sit higher, device is wider. Leaving the iPad
set on v1.2 artwork while the iPhone set is redesigned would make the product
page read as half-finished.

## Final checks before upload

1. View the set at App Store thumbnail size — headlines legible? icons
   recognisable?
2. Swipe through in order: device position must not drift between panels 1, 2,
   3, 4 and 6. Panel 5 is intentionally different.
3. Proofread every headline character by character.
4. Confirm no panel contains a fake phone or fake UI.
5. Confirm the streak count on panel 5 is plausible, not a fresh install.
